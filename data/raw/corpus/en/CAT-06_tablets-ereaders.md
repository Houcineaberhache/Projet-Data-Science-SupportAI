# Product guide — Tablets and e-readers

## 1. Available ranges

| Reference | Model | Screen | Storage | Price | Warranty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TB-601 | NovaPad 10.5 | 10.5" LCD | 128 GB | 2 199 MAD | 12 months |
| TB-602 | NovaPad Pro 12.9 | 12.9" OLED | 256 GB | 4 999 MAD | 12 months |
| TB-603 | NovaPad Mini 8.3 | 8.3" LCD | 64 GB | 1 799 MAD | 12 months |

## 2. Recommended uses

- **TB-601**: family use, streaming, document reading.
- **TB-602**: graphic creation, handwritten notes, multitasking.
- **TB-603**: reading, travel, mobile use.

The stylus is supported on TB-602 only, sold separately (399 MAD).

## 3. Connectivity

Wi-Fi 6 across the range. The cellular version is not offered in Morocco. Bluetooth 5.2, USB-C port for charging and peripherals.

## 4. Box contents

Tablet, USB-C cable, documentation. The power adapter is not included. The keyboard and stylus are sold separately.

## 5. Sales conditions

12-month warranty. Change-of-mind return is possible within 14 days if the screen has not been scratched and the device has been reset. A cracked screen excludes both the warranty and the return.

## 6. Frequently asked questions

**Can I insert a SIM card?** No, the range is Wi-Fi only.

**Is the storage expandable?** Yes, via microSD up to 512 GB on TB-601 and TB-603. Not expandable on TB-602.

**Can I connect a mouse?** Yes, by Bluetooth or through a USB-C hub.

**Is the tablet suitable for children?** Yes, a family mode limits screen time and access to applications.

## 7. E-readers

NovaShop does not sell e-ink e-readers. Tablets can run a reading application, with less comfort than an e-ink screen and shorter battery life.
