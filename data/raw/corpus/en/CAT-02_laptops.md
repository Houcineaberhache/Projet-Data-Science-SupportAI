## 2. Choosing by use case

- **Office work and studies**: Essential 15, sufficient for office suites and browsing.
- **Versatility**: Air 14, best battery life to weight ratio.
- **Creation and development**: Pro 15, OLED screen and 32 GB of RAM.
- **Premium mobility**: Ultra 13, under 1.2 kg.

## 3. Battery life and charging

| Model | Rated battery life | Charger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Air 14 | 12 h | USB-C 65 W |
| Pro 15 | 9 h | USB-C 100 W |
| Essential 15 | 7 h | 45 W mains adapter |
| Ultra 13 | 11 h | USB-C 65 W |

Actual battery life depends on usage (brightness, video, processor load).

## 4. System and software

The laptops are supplied without a pre-installed operating system, unless stated otherwise on the product page. No installation key is provided. Installing an incompatible system may affect the software warranty without voiding the hardware warranty.

## 5. Upgrades and maintenance

- RAM is soldered on Air 14 and Ultra 13: not upgradeable.
- SSD is replaceable on Pro 15 and Essential 15.
- Fan cleaning is recommended every 12 months in dusty environments.

## 6. Sales conditions

12-month commercial warranty, excluding accidental damage. Change-of-mind return is possible within 14 days if the product has not been activated or registered. A product whose system has been installed and personalised remains eligible, but must be reset before return.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**Is a bag included?** No, no carrying accessory is supplied.

**Can I have more RAM installed?** Only on Pro 15 and Essential 15, by an approved technician.

**Is the battery covered?** Yes, if its capacity drops below 80 % during the warranty.

**Can I use the USB-C port for charging?** Yes on Air 14, Pro 15 and Ultra 13 (compatible USB-PD chargers).
