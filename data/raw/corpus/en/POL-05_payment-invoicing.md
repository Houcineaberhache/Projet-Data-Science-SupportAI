# Payment and invoicing policy — NovaShop

**Version:** 1.0 · **Effective date:** 1 September 2026

## 1. Currency and prices

All prices are displayed in **Moroccan dirhams (MAD)**, including all taxes. Delivery fees are added at checkout and displayed before confirmation.

NovaShop reserves the right to change its prices at any time. The applicable price is the one displayed when the order is confirmed.

## 2. Accepted payment methods

| Method | Availability | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bank card (CMI) | Online 24/7 | Visa, Mastercard, CMI |
| Cash on delivery (COD) | Nationwide | +25 MAD, limit 5 000 MAD |
| Bank transfer | Online | Order shipped once funds are received |
| In-store payment | Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech | Cash or card |
| Instalment payment | Orders above 2 000 MAD | Approved partner, 3x or 4x interest-free |

NovaShop does not accept cryptocurrencies, personal cheques or payment by external link.

## 3. Payment security

Card payments are processed by the **CMI** provider through a secure page. NovaShop never stores the full card number or the security code. Transactions are protected by 3-D Secure when the customer's bank offers it.

## 4. Cash on delivery

Cash on delivery is capped at **5 000 MAD** per order. The customer pays in cash or by card to the carrier. The 25 MAD service fee is not refunded in case of a change-of-mind return.

An order refused on delivery without reason may lead to temporary suspension of this payment method for the customer account.

## 5. Instalment payment

Payment in 3x or 4x interest-free is offered for orders above 2 000 MAD, subject to approval by the financial partner. An identity document and proof of income may be requested. If the partner declines, the customer may pay in a single instalment.

## 6. Invoicing

The invoice is issued automatically after shipment and is available in the customer account under "My invoices", in PDF format.

The invoice states: company name, NovaShop ICE number, order identifier, date, item description, net and gross amounts, and applicable VAT.

## 7. Request for a specific invoice

The customer may request an invoice in the name of their company with an ICE number within **30 days** of purchase, using the contact form. After that date, a corrected invoice can no longer be issued.

## 8. Invoicing error

If the invoiced amount differs from the amount paid, the customer must contact support within 30 days. NovaShop makes an adjustment within 10 business days.

## 9. Refunds

Refunds follow the dedicated policy (POL-02). The 3 to 7 business day bank processing time is added to the internal processing time.

## 10. Payment disputes

In case of an unrecognised charge, the customer must first contact their bank to identify the transaction, then NovaShop support with the order identifier. A dispute does not suspend the standard refund procedure.
