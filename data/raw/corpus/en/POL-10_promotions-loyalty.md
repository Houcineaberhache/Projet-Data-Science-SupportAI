# Promotions, promo codes and loyalty programme

**Version:** 1.0 · **Effective date:** 1 September 2026

## 1. Offer types

NovaShop offers four families of offers:

1. **Permanent discounts**: crossed-out prices, bundles and multipacks.
2. **Promotional codes**: percentage or fixed-amount discount.
3. **Event sales**: seasonal sales, Black Friday, back-to-school, holidays.
4. **NovaPoints loyalty programme**.

## 2. General promo code rules

- One code per order.
- Codes cannot be combined with each other.
- A code does not apply to gift cards or to products marked "unbeatable price".
- Each code has a validity date, a minimum purchase amount and sometimes an eligible category.
- Codes are case-sensitive and contain no spaces.

## 3. Example codes

| Code | Benefit | Conditions | Validity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `BIENVENUE10` | -10 % | First order, minimum 300 MAD | Permanent |
| `LIVRAISON0` | Free delivery | Minimum 400 MAD | 31/12/2026 |
| `AUDIO50` | -50 MAD | Audio category, minimum 600 MAD | 30/11/2026 |
| `RENTREE200` | -200 MAD | Laptops, minimum 3 000 MAD | 30/09/2026 |

## 4. Code refused

A code may be refused for the following reasons:

- expired or non-existent code;
- basket below the required minimum;
- non-eligible product;
- code already used on a previous order;
- combination with another ongoing promotion.

The message displayed on refusal states the reason. Support cannot force the application of a non-eligible code.

## 5. Basket changes after applying a code

If the customer modifies the basket after applying a code, the discount is recalculated automatically. If the basket falls below the required minimum, the code is removed.

## 6. NovaPoints loyalty programme

- **1 point** earned per 10 MAD spent.
- **100 points = 20 MAD** discount.
- Points are credited **15 days** after delivery, to account for the return window.
- Points expire **24 months** after they are earned.
- Points cannot be exchanged for cash.

| Tier | Annual threshold | Benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classic | 0 MAD | 1 point / 10 MAD |
| Silver | 5 000 MAD | 1.25 points / 10 MAD |
| Gold | 15 000 MAD | 1.5 points / 10 MAD + free delivery |

Points are cancelled in the event of a product return or a full refund.

## 7. Pricing error

In the event of an obvious pricing error (for example an item at 10 MAD instead of 1 000 MAD), NovaShop may cancel the order and fully refund the customer, in accordance with POL-08, section 6.

## 8. Gift cards

Gift cards are valid for 12 months, can be used in several transactions, are not refundable in cash and cannot be topped up. They can be combined with a promo code.

## 9. Promotional dispute

Any dispute must be sent within **30 days** of the order, with a screenshot of the offer. Support checks the campaign concerned and replies within 5 business days.
