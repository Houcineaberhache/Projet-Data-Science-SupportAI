# Regulatory summary — consumer protection

**Internal summary document. Not legal advice.**

## 1. Reference text

- **Law No. 31-08** enacting consumer protection measures.
- Promulgated by dahir No. 1-11-03 of 18 February 2011.
- Published in **Official Bulletin No. 5932 of 7 April 2011**.
- Text available from the General Secretariat of the Government and institutional portals.

## 2. Principles relevant to customer support

The law sets out several obligations that translate directly into our procedures:

| Legal obligation | Operational translation | Internal document |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clear, prior information to the consumer | Product pages, tax-inclusive prices, delivery fees shown before payment | POL-03, POL-05, POL-07 |
| Regulation of distance selling | Withdrawal period, information on return conditions | POL-01, POL-08 |
| Prohibition of unfair terms | Review of the terms, no clause limiting mandatory rights | POL-07 |
| Conformity of the delivered product | Handling of conformity defects | POL-04, POL-09 |
| Right to a warranty | Distinction between legal and commercial warranty | POL-04, REG-02 |
| Protection against misleading advertising | Accuracy of product pages and promotions | POL-10 |

## 3. Right of withdrawal

The regulations frame withdrawal in the context of distance selling. NovaShop applies a commercial period of **14 calendar days**, presented as a commercial choice made by NovaShop.

**Point of attention:** the exact legal period, its exceptions and how it is calculated must be confirmed against the official text before any customer communication. The current wording of POL-01 is cautious and does not assert a legal figure.

## 4. Usual exceptions to returns

Without prejudging the exact text, the following categories are generally excluded from change-of-mind returns: personalised goods, perishable goods, sealed products opened for hygiene reasons, digital content already executed.

NovaShop applies these exceptions in POL-01, section 4.

## 5. Consumer remedies

If a dispute cannot be resolved amicably, the consumer may refer the matter to the competent authorities and to consumer protection associations. NovaShop must inform the customer of this possibility without conditions.

Support must never:

- state that a remedy is impossible;
- make a refund conditional on waiving a remedy;
- advise the customer not to report a dispute.

## 6. What the assistant may say

- "Our commercial policy provides a 14-day period."
- "This product is not among the categories eligible for a change-of-mind return."
- "If you contest this decision, you may request a review and, if necessary, refer the matter to the competent authorities."

## 7. What the assistant must not say

- Quote an article number of the law that has not been verified.
- State that a clause is "illegal".
- Give personalised legal interpretation.
- Promise the outcome of a dispute.

## 8. Monitoring

 Any change to the reference text must trigger a version update and a new review.
