# Shipping and delivery policy — NovaShop

**Version:** 1.0 · **Effective date:** 1 September 2026

## 1. Areas served

NovaShop delivers throughout Morocco through its network of logistics partners. Areas are grouped into three categories:

| Area | Main cities | Indicative time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Area A | Casablanca, Rabat, Salé, Témara, Mohammedia | 24 to 48 hours |
| Area B | Marrakech, Fès, Tanger, Agadir, Meknès, Oujda, Kénitra, Tétouan | 2 to 4 business days |
| Area C | Other cities and rural areas | 3 to 6 business days |

Times run from payment confirmation, not from order placement. An order placed after 16:00 or on a Sunday is processed on the next business day.

## 2. Delivery fees

| Order value | Standard fee |
| --- | --- |
| Below 500 MAD | 39 MAD |
| 500 MAD and above | Free |
| Area C | 49 MAD (free from 800 MAD) |
| Cash on delivery | +25 MAD service fee |

Fees are displayed before order confirmation. No additional fee may be requested at the time of delivery.

## 3. Preparation and dispatch

- Warehouse preparation: 2 to 24 business hours.
- A tracking number is sent by email and SMS as soon as the parcel is handed to the carrier.
- Orders containing several items may be shipped in several parcels, at no extra cost.

## 4. Home delivery

The carrier contacts the customer by phone before delivery. The customer must be reachable and present an identity document for orders above 3 000 MAD.

After **two failed attempts**, the parcel is returned to the warehouse. The customer is then contacted to choose between a new shipment (39 MAD fee) and a refund (initial delivery fees not refunded).

## 5. Store pickup

Free pickup is available in the Casablanca, Rabat and Marrakech stores. The customer receives a pickup code and has **7 days** to collect the order. After that, the order is refunded or reshipped at the customer's request.

## 6. Delivery delay

A parcel is considered late when the maximum time for its area has been exceeded by **3 business days**. The customer may then:

1. request a carrier investigation (answer within 48 hours);
2. cancel and obtain a full refund if the parcel cannot be located;
3. wait for delivery with a 50 MAD store credit as compensation.

In case of delay caused by exceptional circumstances (strike, severe weather, force majeure), NovaShop informs the customer and offers a new deadline or a refund, without penalty.

## 7. Damaged or opened parcel

The customer must refuse a parcel that is visibly damaged or opened and report it immediately to the carrier. If the damage is noticed after opening, the customer has **48 hours** to send photos to support. See POL-09.

## 8. Incorrect address

NovaShop is not responsible for delays or losses caused by an incorrect address or phone number provided by the customer. Reshipping is charged 39 MAD.

## 9. Relay points

A **relay point** is a partner shop (newsagent, convenience store, local shop) approved by NovaShop for parcel drop-off and collection. It allows delivery when the customer is away from home and return drop-off without visiting a NovaShop store.

- The customer selects the relay point at checkout from those offered for their city.
- The parcel is held for **5 business days** from the moment it becomes available.
- Collection requires the order number and an identity document.
- Parcels exceeding 20 kg or 100 cm on one side are not accepted at relay points.
- After the holding period, the parcel returns to the warehouse and the customer is contacted under the conditions of section 4.

The relay point is also the return drop-off method described in POL-01, section 6, and in PRO-02, section 6.
