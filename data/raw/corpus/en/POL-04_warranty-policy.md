# Warranty and after-sales policy — NovaShop

**Version:** 1.0 · **Effective date:** 1 September 2026

## 1. Two separate warranties

Every product sold by NovaShop benefits from two protections:

1. **The legal warranty**, imposed by Moroccan consumer protection regulations, which covers defects of conformity.
2. **The NovaShop commercial warranty**, offered by the seller or the manufacturer, whose duration varies by category.

These two warranties do not replace one another. See REG-02 for the regulatory summary.

## 2. Commercial warranty periods

| Category | Duration | Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Smartphones | 12 months | Manufacturer + NovaShop handling |
| Laptops | 12 months | Manufacturer |
| Audio (headphones, earbuds, speakers) | 12 months | Manufacturer |
| Computer accessories | 6 months | NovaShop |
| Smartwatches | 12 months | Manufacturer |
| Tablets | 12 months | Manufacturer |
| Batteries and cables | 3 months | NovaShop |

The duration is stated on the invoice and on the product page. It runs from the delivery date.

## 3. What is covered

- Manufacturing defects and hidden defects.
- Hardware failure occurring during normal use.
- A battery whose capacity drops below 80 % during the warranty period.
- Assembly defects, faulty screen without impact, defective button or connector.

## 4. What is not covered

- Fall, impact, pressure or cracked screen.
- Liquid ingress, corrosion, humidity.
- Overvoltage, lightning, unsuitable power supply.
- Repair or modification by a non-approved third party.
- Normal wear (cosmetic scratches, reduced battery life with age).
- Loss or theft.
- Software, viruses and personal data.

## 5. Claim procedure

1. The customer opens an after-sales case from the customer account or contacts support with the order number.
2. Support checks the purchase date and the warranty period.
3. The customer drops the product off in store or receives a prepaid return label.
4. Diagnosis is carried out within **5 business days**.
5. NovaShop offers a repair, a replacement or a store credit depending on the diagnosis.

## 6. Repair times

| Situation | Indicative time |
| --- | --- |
| Accessory replaceable from stock | 48 hours |
| Repair at partner workshop | 7 to 14 business days |
| Return to manufacturer | 14 to 21 business days |

If the repair exceeds 21 business days, the customer may request a replacement or a full refund.

## 7. Non-repairable product

If the product cannot be repaired and no replacement stock is available, NovaShop offers a store credit increased by 5 % or a refund proportional to the residual value, depending on how long the product has been used.

## 8. Dead on arrival (DOA)

A product reported faulty within **48 hours** of delivery is handled as "Dead On Arrival": priority replacement at no cost, subject to stock availability and verification of the fault. See POL-09.

## 9. Proof of purchase

The invoice or the order number serves as proof of purchase. A product bought second-hand or from a non-approved third party is not covered by the NovaShop warranty.
