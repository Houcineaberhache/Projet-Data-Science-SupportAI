# Procedure — Returning a product

**Audience:** customer · **Estimated duration:** 10 minutes of preparation

## 1. Before you start

Check the following three points:

1. The order is **less than 14 calendar days** old (POL-01).
2. The product is complete and in its original packaging.
3. The product is not in a category excluded from change-of-mind returns.

## 2. Opening the request

**Option A — Customer account**
1. Sign in at novashop.ma.
2. Go to "My orders".
3. Click "Request a return".
4. Select the items and the reason.
5. Confirm the request.

**Option B — By email**
Send a message to `retours@novashop.ma` with the order number, the item concerned and the reason.

**Option C — In store**
Go to the reception desk with the invoice or the order number.

## 3. Accepted return reasons

| Code | Reason | Fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `R01` | Change of mind | 39 MAD |
| `R02` | Faulty product | Free |
| `R03` | Wrong product | Free |
| `R04` | Product damaged on delivery | Free — handled under POL-09 |
| `R05` | Missing item | Free |
| `R06` | Not as described | Free |

## 4. Receiving the return authorisation

Support checks eligibility and issues a return authorisation `RET-XXXXXX` within **24 business hours**. This authorisation contains:

- the return number;
- the drop-off address or relay point;
- packaging instructions;
- the prepaid label, if the return is free.

## 5. Preparing the parcel

1. Remove personal data from the device and restore factory settings.
2. Place the device, accessories and manual in the original box.
3. Insert the printed return authorisation or the return code.
4. Close the parcel securely and attach the label.

NovaShop accepts no liability for data loss. Backing up data beforehand is the customer's responsibility.

## 6. Dropping off the parcel

- **Relay point:** hand over against receipt, within 5 business days of the authorisation being issued (definition: POL-03, section 9).
- **Store:** hand over at reception, with a visual check.
- **Home collection:** available in areas A and B, by appointment.

## 7. Quality inspection

On receipt, the team checks:

- the serial number matches;
- the general condition and absence of damage;
- the presence of all accessories;
- the absence of residual personal data.

The inspection takes **48 hours**. The customer is informed of the result by email.

## 8. After the inspection

| Result | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| Compliant | Refund or exchange initiated (POL-02) |
| Compliant with reservation | Partial refund after customer agreement |
| Non-compliant | Return refused, product shipped back to the customer |

## 9. Summary of times

| Step | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Request validation | 24 business hours |
| Return transport | 2 to 5 business days |
| Quality inspection | 48 h |
| Refund | 2 business days |
| Bank credit | 3 to 7 business days |
