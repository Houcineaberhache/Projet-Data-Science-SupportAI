# Returns and exchanges policy — NovaShop

**Version:** 1.0 · **Effective date:** 1 September 2026
**Scope:** orders placed on novashop.ma and in NovaShop stores (Morocco).

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the conditions under which a customer may return or exchange a product purchased from NovaShop. It applies to every category sold: smartphones, laptops, audio, computer accessories, smartwatches and tablets.

## 2. Return window

The customer has **14 calendar days** from the date of delivery to request a return. This period is a commercial choice made by NovaShop. It does not affect the legal guarantees described in the warranty policy (POL-04).

The delivery date recorded by the carrier counts as day 0. If the 14th day falls on a Sunday or a public holiday, the deadline is extended to the next business day.

## 3. Eligibility conditions

A product is eligible for return only if all of the following are met:

1. The request is submitted within the 14-day window.
2. The product is complete: device, original accessories, manuals, cables and box.
3. The product has not been significantly used (no scratches, no signs of wear, no personal data left on the device).
4. The original packaging is present and in acceptable condition.
5. The customer provides the order number and proof of purchase.

For sealed products (cartridges, consumables, hygiene items), a return is accepted only if the seal is intact.

## 4. Non-eligible products

The following products cannot be returned as a change of mind:

- Products personalised or engraved at the customer's request.
- Software, digital licences and prepaid cards that have already been activated.
- Products bought during clearance sales explicitly marked "final sale".
- Products damaged by misuse, a fall, contact with liquid, or a repair carried out outside the approved network.

A faulty product remains covered by the warranty even if it is no longer eligible for a change-of-mind return. A product **damaged in transit** does not fall under change-of-mind returns: it is handled by the delivery incident procedure (POL-09).

## 5. Return costs

- Change-of-mind return: **39 MAD** shipping fee, deducted from the refund.
- Return of a faulty, wrong or damaged-on-delivery product: **free**, upon presentation of photos. A product damaged in transit is handled under POL-09.
- In-store return: **free**, no shipping fee.

## 6. Procedure

1. Open a request from the customer account, under "My orders", or contact support.
2. Support checks eligibility and issues a return authorisation (RET-XXXXXX) within 24 business hours.
3. The customer drops the parcel at a relay point (definition: POL-03, section 9) or hands the product over in store.
4. The quality team inspects the product within 48 hours of receipt.
5. The refund or exchange is initiated according to the refund policy (POL-02).

## 7. Partial returns

An order containing several items may be returned in full or in part.

- The customer may return a single item: they select the relevant items when requesting the return.
- The refund covers only the items actually returned (POL-02, section 6).
- Initial delivery fees are not recalculated and are not refunded, unless the incident is attributable to NovaShop.
- The 39 MAD return fee applies once per request, regardless of the number of items returned.

**Exception:** a product sold as a bundle must be returned in full. Returning a single component does not entitle the customer to a refund of the bundle.

## 8. Exchanges

An exchange is possible for a product of equal or higher value, subject to stock availability. If the new product is more expensive, the difference is paid by card or cash on delivery. If it is cheaper, the difference is refunded or converted into store credit.

## 9. Contact

Returns department: `retours@novashop.ma` — Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 19:00.
