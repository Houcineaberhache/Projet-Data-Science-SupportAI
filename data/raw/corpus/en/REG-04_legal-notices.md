## 3. Purpose of the site

novashop.ma is an online store for electronic products and accessories intended for consumers in Morocco. The site allows catalogue browsing, ordering, delivery tracking and returns management.

## 4. Intellectual property

The content of the site (texts, visuals, logos, structure) is the property of NovaShop or its partners. Unauthorised reproduction is prohibited, except for short quotations with mention of the source.

Manufacturer trademarks cited belong to their respective owners.

## 5. Framework applicable to e-commerce

E-commerce in Morocco falls in particular within:

- law No. 31-08 on consumer protection (prior information, withdrawal, conformity);
- law No. 09-08 on the protection of personal data;
- law No. 53-05 on electronic exchange of legal data, which frames electronic signatures and the evidential value of electronic documents.

**Point of attention:** the exact list and scope of these texts change over time; this document is an educational summary.

## 6. Mandatory information before ordering

In accordance with consumer information requirements, the site displays before confirmation:

- the identity of the seller and contact details;
- the essential features of the product;
- the price in MAD including all taxes;
- delivery fees and times;
- return and warranty conditions;
- accepted payment methods.

## 7. Complaints

Complaints may be sent to `support@novashop.ma`. A written answer is provided within 10 business days. External remedies are described in REG-01.

## 8. Mediation and dispute resolution

NovaShop favours amicable resolution. If this fails, the consumer may use the mechanisms provided by Moroccan regulations. No clause of the terms and conditions limits this right.
