# Damaged, wrong or missing item

**Version:** 1.0 · **Effective date:** 1 September 2026

This document covers incidents occurring **at delivery or before receipt**. It does not cover failures occurring after use, which are handled by the warranty (POL-04).

## 1. Parcel damaged on delivery

The customer must **refuse the parcel** if the packaging is visibly torn, crushed, wet or resealed. The carrier records the refusal and the parcel returns to the warehouse.

If the customer accepts the parcel, they have **48 hours** to report the damage to support, with:

1. the order number;
2. at least three photos (packaging, label, product);
3. a description of the damage.

After 48 hours, the damage is presumed to have occurred after delivery and the claim falls under the warranty, subject to acceptance of the case.

## 2. Wrong item

If the delivered product does not match the order (reference, colour, capacity, model), the customer must report it within **7 days**. NovaShop arranges the free return and ships the correct product as a priority.

If the ordered product is no longer available, the customer chooses between an equivalent product, a store credit increased by 5 %, or a full refund.

## 3. Missing item

A missing item must be reported within **48 hours** of delivery. NovaShop checks the packing slip and the parcel weight. If the shortage is confirmed, the item is shipped free of charge within 72 business hours.

The customer must keep the packaging until the case is resolved.

## 4. Parcel not received

A parcel is declared not received when:

- tracking shows "delivered" but the customer received nothing;
- tracking has been stuck for more than 5 business days;
- the carrier returned the parcel to the warehouse.

Support then opens a carrier investigation. The answer is provided within **48 business hours**. Three outcomes are possible:

| Investigation outcome | Proposed solution |
| --- | --- |
| Parcel found | Immediate reshipment, fees waived |
| Parcel lost, carrier responsibility | Replacement or full refund |
| Parcel delivered to another address by customer error | Reshipment charged 39 MAD |

## 5. Fraud and false claims

Every loss or damage report is verified. In the event of a proven fraudulent claim, NovaShop may refuse the claim, close the case and suspend the account.

## 6. Dead on arrival (DOA)

A defect noticed within 48 hours is handled as a priority "Dead On Arrival" product: free replacement if stock allows, after a quick in-store diagnosis. See POL-04, section 8.

## 7. Refund of fees

When an incident is attributable to NovaShop or the carrier, all fees (initial delivery, return, reshipment) are borne by NovaShop.

## 8. Escalation

A case unresolved after 5 business days is automatically escalated to support level 2 (see OPS-01).
