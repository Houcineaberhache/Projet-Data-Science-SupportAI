# FAQ — Promotions, promo codes and loyalty

## Where can I find current promo codes?

Active offers are displayed on the site's "Promotions" page and in the newsletter. Event codes are communicated by email and on NovaShop's official social media accounts.

## Why is my promo code refused?

Common reasons: expired or non-existent code, basket below the required minimum, non-eligible product, code already used, or combination with another promotion. The message displayed on refusal states the reason.

## Can I combine several promo codes?

No, one code per order. However, a gift card can be combined with a promo code.

## Does the promo code apply to discounted products?

Each code states its exclusions. By default, a code does not apply to gift cards or to products marked "unbeatable price".

## How does the loyalty programme work?

You earn 1 point per 10 MAD spent. 100 points equal a 20 MAD discount. Points are credited 15 days after delivery and expire 24 months after being earned.

## How do I use my points?

At checkout, enable the "Use my NovaPoints" option. The discount is applied automatically within the basket amount. Points cannot be converted into cash.

## Were my points cancelled after a return?

Yes, points earned for a returned or refunded product are cancelled. If the points have already been used, the balance may become negative and will be offset against future purchases.

## How do I move up the loyalty tiers?

The tier depends on the amount accumulated over 12 months: Classic by default, Silver from 5 000 MAD, Gold from 15 000 MAD. The benefits are detailed in the loyalty policy (POL-10).

## Do gift cards expire?

Yes, gift cards are valid for 12 months from activation. They can be used in several transactions, cannot be topped up and are not refundable in cash.

## Can I get a refund for a product bought on promotion?

Yes, the refund is calculated on the price actually paid after discount, not on the crossed-out price.

## What should I do if a promotion was not applied?

Contact support within 30 days of the order and attach a screenshot of the offer. An adjustment is possible within 5 business days if the offer was indeed active.

## Are the prices displayed during sales guaranteed?

Displayed prices are valid for the duration of the campaign, while stocks last. An obvious pricing error may lead to cancellation of the order and a full refund.
