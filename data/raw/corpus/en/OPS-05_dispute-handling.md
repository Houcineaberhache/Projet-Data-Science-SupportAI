# Dispute and complaint handling procedure

**Internal document.**

## 1. Definition

A dispute is a persistent disagreement between the customer and NovaShop after a first support answer. A complaint is a formal request for review.

A case becomes a dispute only after **two exchanges** that did not lead to an accepted solution.

## 2. Dispute classification

| Type | Example | Level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L1 — Informational | Disagreement over an announced deadline | L1 |
| L2 — Financial | Contested refund, incorrect amount | L2 |
| L3 — Quality | Non-compliant product, contested diagnosis | L2 |
| L4 — Legal | Threat of proceedings, consumer association | L3 |
| L5 — Security | Compromised data, fraud | L3 + DPO |

## 3. Processing steps

1. **Receipt**: case recorded with an identifier `LIT-XXXXXX`.
2. **Classification**: assignment of a type from L1 to L5 within 24 business hours.
3. **Investigation**: collection of documents, verification of facts, review of policies.
4. **Decision**: written and reasoned answer, referencing the applicable policy.
5. **Closure**: customer agreement or escalation to the next level.

## 4. Times

| Step | Maximum time |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledgement of receipt | 24 business hours |
| Classification | 24 business hours |
| Investigation | 10 business days |
| Written decision | 10 business days |
| Internal appeal | 15 business days |

## 5. Content of the written decision

The answer must contain: a reminder of the facts, the policy applied with its reference, the decision, the reason for refusal where applicable, the route of appeal and the deadline.

No decision may be based on a rule that is absent from the official documentation.

## 6. Goodwill gestures

| Amount | Approval required |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 100 MAD | L1 |
| 100 to 500 MAD | L2 |
| Above 500 MAD | L3 |

A goodwill gesture does not constitute an admission of liability and must be recorded.

## 7. External recourse

If the dispute persists, the customer is informed of the remedies provided by Moroccan consumer protection regulations (see REG-01). NovaShop cannot make the exercise of a remedy conditional on waiving a right.

## 8. Indicators

- Number of disputes per month and per type.
- Amicable resolution rate.
- Average decision time.
- Average cost of goodwill gestures.

## 9. Archiving

Each dispute is kept for 5 years in the ticketing system, with all exchanges and documents. Personal data is processed in accordance with POL-06.
