# FAQ — Products, stock and buying advice

## How do I know if a product is in stock?

Stock is displayed on the product page. "In stock" means the item is available for immediate dispatch. "Limited stock" means fewer than 5 units. "Out of stock" means no order is possible; you can request a restock alert.

## Are the products new?

Yes, all products sold by NovaShop are new and sealed, unless explicitly marked "refurbished" on the product page. Refurbished products benefit from a 6-month warranty and are clearly labelled.

## Are the products compatible with Moroccan networks?

The smartphones sold are compatible with the 2G, 3G and 4G bands of Moroccan operators. 5G compatibility depends on the model and the operator's coverage; it is stated on each product page.

## Are the products genuine and covered by the manufacturer?

Yes, NovaShop sources from approved distributors. Each product comes with an invoice and, where applicable, a manufacturer warranty card.

## Can I see the product before buying?

Yes, display units are available in the Casablanca, Rabat and Marrakech stores. Some high-demand models may not be on display; contact the store to check availability.

## Do you offer advice on choosing a product?

Yes. The online chat provides a recommendation based on your budget and usage. The category guides (CAT-01 to CAT-06) present the ranges, key features and use cases.

## Will an out-of-stock product be restocked?

Most stock-outs are restocked within 2 to 4 weeks. Enable the restock alert to be notified by email when the item is back. No deadline is guaranteed for end-of-life products.

## Can I order a product that is not listed on the site?

NovaShop does not offer special orders for individuals. Businesses may contact the sales department for volume requirements.

## Are prices negotiable?

No. Displayed prices are fixed, except for ongoing promotional campaigns. Stores apply the same prices as the website, excluding delivery fees.

## Are accessories included?

The box contents are stated on each product page. Generally: device, charging cable, documentation. The power adapter and earphones are not always included, depending on the manufacturer.

## Can I test a product before returning it?

A product may be tried out to check that it works, but significant use may make a change-of-mind return impossible. Test without removing protective films and keep the packaging.

## How do I report an error on a product page?

Use the "Report an error" link on the page, or contact support stating the product identifier. A correction is made within 5 business days.
