# Personal data protection policy — NovaShop

**Version:** 1.0 · **Effective date:** 1 September 2026
**Demonstration document: fictional data.**

## 1. Data controller

NovaShop SARL, a fictional company created for this educational project, is the controller of the data collected on novashop.ma. Any request relating to personal data may be sent to `dpo@novashop.ma`.

## 2. Data collected

| Purpose | Data | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account creation and management | First name, last name, email, encrypted password | Contract |
| Order processing | Address, phone number, purchase history | Contract |
| Delivery | Address, phone number, recipient name | Contract |
| Invoicing | ICE number, billing address | Legal obligation |
| Customer support | Content of exchanges, order identifier | Legitimate interest |
| Marketing | Email, preferences | Consent |

NovaShop does not collect sensitive data (health, origin, religion, political opinions) and never asks for a card number by email or phone.

## 3. Retention periods

| Data type | Duration |
| --- | --- |
| Active account data | For the lifetime of the account |
| Inactive account | 3 years after the last login |
| Order and invoice data | 10 years (accounting obligation) |
| Support records | 2 years |
| Marketing data | 3 years after the last consent |
| Audience measurement cookies | 13 months maximum |

## 4. Recipients

Data is accessible only to authorised employees and to the subcontractors required for the service: carrier, payment provider, hosting provider and support platform vendor. These subcontractors are bound by a confidentiality agreement and may not use the data for other purposes.

NovaShop does not sell or rent personal data.

## 5. Transfers outside Morocco

Some technical providers may host data outside Morocco. In that case, NovaShop ensures that the destination country offers an adequate level of protection or puts appropriate contractual safeguards in place.

## 6. Data subject rights

In accordance with Moroccan law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data, the customer has the following rights:

- right of access to their data;
- right of rectification;
- right to object on legitimate grounds;
- right to erasure, within the limits of legal retention obligations;
- right to withdraw consent at any time for marketing.

A request is processed within **30 days**. An identity document may be requested to verify the identity of the applicant.

## 7. Cookies

The site uses three categories of cookies: strictly necessary, audience measurement and personalisation. Non-essential cookies require explicit consent, which can be changed at any time from the cookie management banner.

## 8. Security

NovaShop applies TLS encryption on all pages, password hashing, role-based restriction of internal access, and logging of customer data consultations.

## 9. Data breach

In the event of a breach likely to affect data subjects' rights, NovaShop notifies the competent authority and informs the data subjects as soon as possible.

## 10. Complaints

In case of disagreement, the customer may refer the matter to the **CNDP** (National Commission for the Control of Personal Data Protection). See REG-03.
