## 2. Noise reduction

Only the AU-301 model has active noise cancellation (ANC). NovaBuds Pro offer passive isolation reinforced by foam tips. NovaBuds Lite and the Mini speaker have no noise reduction.

## 3. Pairing

1. Enable Bluetooth on the phone.
2. Hold the power button for 3 seconds until it flashes.
3. Select "NovaSound" or "NovaBuds" in the device list.
4. If pairing fails, remove the device from the list and start again.

Only one device can be connected at a time on the Lite models. Multipoint (2 devices) is available on AU-301 and AU-302.

## 4. Care and safety

- Clean the tips with warm water without soap, then dry completely.
- Do not expose the earbuds to temperatures above 45 °C.
- Water resistance (IPX4) protects against splashes, not immersion.
- The warranty excludes damage related to moisture and sweat on non-certified models.

## 5. Sales conditions

12-month commercial warranty. For hygiene reasons, in-ear earbuds are accepted for return only if the seal is intact. A manufacturing defect found on receipt remains covered by the warranty.

## 6. Frequently asked questions

**Can I use the headphones on a plane?** Yes, a 3.5 mm jack cable is supplied with AU-301.

**Are the earbuds compatible with iPhone?** Yes, over Bluetooth. Apple's proprietary chip is not supported.

**How do I update the firmware?** Through the companion app, available on iOS and Android.

**Is the charging case covered?** Yes, 12 months, excluding accidental damage.
