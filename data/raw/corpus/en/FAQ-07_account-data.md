# FAQ — Customer account and personal data

## How do I create an account?

Click "My account" then "Create an account". Enter your name, email and a password of at least 8 characters. A verification email is sent; the account becomes active after confirmation.

## I forgot my password, what should I do?

On the login page, click "Forgot password". Enter your email: a reset link valid for 1 hour is sent. If the email does not arrive, check the spam folder then contact support.

## How do I delete my account?

From "My account" then "Privacy", click "Delete my account". Deletion takes effect within 30 days. Some data (invoices, accounting history) is kept for 10 years in accordance with legal obligations.

## What data do you collect?

First name, last name, email, address, phone number, order history and the content of exchanges with support. No sensitive data is collected and NovaShop never asks for a card number by email or phone.

## Is my data resold?

No. NovaShop does not sell or rent personal data. It is shared only with the subcontractors required for the order: carrier, payment provider, hosting provider.

## How long is my data kept?

Account data is kept for the lifetime of the account, then 3 years after the last login. Invoices are kept for 10 years. Support records are kept for 2 years.

## How do I exercise my rights of access or rectification?

Send a request to `dpo@novashop.ma` stating your identity. An answer is provided within 30 days. An identity document may be requested to verify the identity of the applicant.

## Can I object to marketing?

Yes. Consent can be withdrawn at any time from "My account" then "Preferences", or via the unsubscribe link in every marketing email. Transactional emails (order, delivery) are still sent.

## How do I manage cookies?

The cookie management banner lets you accept or refuse non-essential cookies. The choice can be changed at any time from the site footer.

## Can I order without giving my phone number?

No, the phone number is required for delivery: the carrier contacts the customer before the visit. It is not used for commercial purposes without consent.

## What should I do in the event of a data breach?

Contact `dpo@novashop.ma` immediately. NovaShop notifies the competent authority and informs the data subjects as soon as possible.

## How do I contact the CNDP?

In the event of a persistent disagreement, you may file a complaint with the National Commission for the Control of Personal Data Protection (CNDP). See REG-03.
