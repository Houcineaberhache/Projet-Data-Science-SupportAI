# Procedure — Requesting a warranty repair

**Audience:** customer · **Prerequisite:** product bought from NovaShop, under warranty

## 1. Checking eligibility

Before opening a case, the customer checks:

1. the purchase date on the invoice;
2. the warranty period for the category (POL-04, section 2);
3. the absence of an exclusion cause (fall, liquid, repair by a third party).

## 2. Opening the after-sales case

**Online:**
1. Sign in to the customer account.
2. Open "My orders" then the relevant order.
3. Click "Request after-sales support".
4. Select the symptom observed.
5. Describe the problem and attach photos or a short video.

**In store:** go to the after-sales desk with the invoice and the product.

The case receives an identifier `SAV-XXXXXX`.

## 3. Suggested symptoms

| Code | Symptom |
| --- | --- |
| `S01` | No longer powers on |
| `S02` | Faulty screen or unresponsive touch |
| `S03` | Battery no longer holds a charge |
| `S04` | No longer charges |
| `S05` | Audio problem (speaker, microphone) |
| `S06` | Connectivity (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, network) |
| `S07` | Overheating or unexpected shutdown |
| `S08` | Other hardware defect |

## 4. Preliminary diagnosis

Support carries out a remote diagnosis (questions, charging test, reset) within **24 business hours**. If the problem is software-related, a solution may be provided without returning the product.

If a hardware defect is confirmed, support issues either:

- a prepaid return label, or
- a store appointment, or
- a home collection for bulky products.

## 5. Drop-off and workshop diagnosis

The product is received and diagnosed within **5 business days**. Three outcomes are possible:

| Outcome | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| Defect covered by warranty | Free repair |
| Defect not covered | Quote sent to the customer, 24 h to accept |
| Non-repairable product | Replacement or store credit according to POL-04, section 7 |

## 6. Out-of-warranty quote

If the damage is not covered, the customer receives a detailed quote. Without a reply within **15 days**, the product is returned as is, at NovaShop's expense, and the case is closed.

## 7. Repair time

| Type | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Accessory in stock | 48 h |
| Partner workshop | 7 to 14 business days |
| Return to manufacturer | 14 to 21 business days |

The customer is notified at every status change.

## 8. Return of the product

The repaired product is delivered in store or at home, free of charge. A service report is provided, stating the parts replaced and the remaining warranty.

The repair is guaranteed for **3 months** for the replaced part.

## 9. Disagreement

If the customer disputes the diagnosis, they may request a **free second opinion**. The answer is provided within 10 business days. If disagreement persists, the case is escalated to level 3 (OPS-01).
