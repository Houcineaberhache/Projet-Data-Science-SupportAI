# Procedure — Contacting support and escalating

**Audience:** customer

## 1. Contact channels

| Channel | Address / number | Hours | Response time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Online chat | novashop.ma | 9:00 – 19:00, Monday to Saturday | Immediate to 5 min |
| Email | `support@novashop.ma` | 24/7 (processed on business days) | 24 business hours |
| Phone | +212 5 22 00 00 00 (fictional) | 9:00 – 19:00, Monday to Saturday | Immediate |
| WhatsApp | +212 6 00 00 00 00 (fictional) | 9:00 – 19:00 | 2 business hours |
| Store | Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech | 10:00 – 20:00, Monday to Saturday | Immediate |

On Sundays and public holidays, only email and the automated chat are available.

## 2. Information to prepare

To speed up processing, the customer prepares:

- the order number (`ORD-XXXXXX`) or case number (`SAV-` / `RET-`);
- the email address used at purchase;
- a precise description of the problem;
- supporting documents (photos, invoice, screenshot).

## 3. Support levels

| Level | Role | Handles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L1 | Support advisor | Common questions, tracking, product information |
| L2 | Specialist | Disputes, complex refunds, carrier incidents |
| L3 | Support manager | Persistent disagreements, goodwill gestures, legal cases |

## 4. Escalation rules

A case is escalated automatically when:

- the resolution time for the current level is exceeded;
- the customer has contested the answer received twice;
- the amount at stake exceeds 3 000 MAD;
- the case presents a legal or reputational risk.

Manual escalation is possible through the internal tool, with a mandatory reason.

## 5. What support cannot do

L1 support cannot:

- modify an order that has already been shipped;
- grant a refund outside policy without L2 approval;
- disclose another customer's data;
- promise a deadline not confirmed by the carrier.

## 6. Requesting human assistance

The customer may ask to speak to a human agent at any time. If no agent is available, a callback is scheduled within 4 business hours, or the case is forwarded by email.

## 7. Ticket tracking

Every exchange creates a ticket with an identifier. The customer follows its progress in the customer account under "My requests". An email is sent with each reply.

## 8. Satisfaction survey

At closure, the customer receives a request to rate the service from 1 to 5. Ratings of 2 or below automatically trigger a quality review.

## 9. Formal complaint

If the customer is not satisfied with the answer, they may file a formal complaint by email with the subject "COMPLAINT". A written answer is provided within **10 business days**. See OPS-05.
