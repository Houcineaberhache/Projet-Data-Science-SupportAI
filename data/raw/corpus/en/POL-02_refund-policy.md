# Refund policy — NovaShop

**Version:** 1.0 · **Effective date:** 1 September 2026

## 1. General principles

NovaShop issues a refund in three situations:

1. A return accepted under the returns policy (POL-01).
2. An order cancelled before shipment (POL-08).
3. A product not delivered or delivered damaged, after the carrier investigation (POL-09).

A refund can never exceed the amount actually paid by the customer, including delivery fees when NovaShop is responsible.

## 2. Processing times

| Step | Maximum time |
| --- | --- |
| Request opened and validated | 24 business hours |
| Returned product received | 2 to 5 business days |
| Quality inspection | 48 hours |
| Refund issued | 2 business days after validation |
| Credit actually visible on the customer account | 3 to 7 business days (bank processing) |

The usual end-to-end time is therefore **7 to 15 business days** between the request and the visible credit. This depends partly on the customer's bank and is outside NovaShop's control.

## 3. Refund methods

| Original payment method | Refund method |
| --- | --- |
| Bank card (CMI) | Refund to the same card |
| Cash on delivery | Bank transfer or store credit |
| Bank transfer | Transfer to the same account |
| Store credit / voucher | New store credit issued |

For cash-on-delivery payments, the customer must provide a valid bank account details form (RIB). Refunds cannot be paid in cash.

## 4. NovaShop store credit

The customer may choose store credit instead of a bank refund. The credit is increased by **5 %** of the refunded amount and remains valid for **12 months**. It can be used in one or several transactions, online and in store, and cannot be converted into cash.

## 5. Deducted fees

For a change-of-mind return, the 39 MAD return shipping fee is deducted from the refunded amount. Initial delivery fees are refunded only if the order is cancelled before shipment or if the error is attributable to NovaShop.

## 6. Special cases

- **Partially returned order:** the refund covers only the returned items. Delivery fees are not recalculated.
- **Product bought with a promo code:** the refund is calculated on the price actually paid, after discount.
- **Gift or bundle:** refunding a bundle requires returning all of its components.
- **Refund refused:** the customer receives a written justification and may dispute it within 30 days.

## 7. No refund

No refund is granted if:

- the returned product does not match the one shipped (different serial number);
- the product was damaged after delivery;
- the return window has expired and no warranty applies;
- the order was paid through a fraudulent method.

## 8. Tracking

The refund status is visible in the customer account under "My refunds". The customer receives an email at every status change. If the delay exceeds 15 business days, support opens a level 2 complaint (OPS-01).
