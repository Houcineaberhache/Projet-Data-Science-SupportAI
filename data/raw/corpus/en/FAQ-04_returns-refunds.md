# FAQ — Returns and refunds

## How long do I have to return a product?

You have **14 calendar days** from receipt of the parcel. The delivery day counts as day 0. If the 14th day falls on a Sunday or a public holiday, the deadline is extended to the next business day.

## What are the conditions for returning a product?

The product must be complete (device, accessories, manuals, box), not significantly used, free of personal data, and accompanied by the order number. Sealed products are accepted only if the seal is intact.

## Which products cannot be returned?

Personalised or engraved products, activated software and digital licences, items marked "final sale" during clearance, and products damaged by misuse or by a repair outside the approved network.

## Can I return a single item from an order containing several products?

Yes. When requesting the return, select only the relevant items: the refund then covers those items only, and the initial delivery fees are not refunded. The 39 MAD return fee applies once per request, regardless of the number of items. A product sold as a bundle must, however, be returned in full (POL-01, section 7).

## Is the return free?

The return is free for a faulty, wrong or damaged-on-delivery product, or a missing item. For a simple change of mind, a 39 MAD shipping fee is deducted from the refund. In-store returns are always free.

## How long does the refund take?

Allow 7 to 15 business days in total: 24 hours for validation, 2 to 5 days for return transport, 48 hours for quality inspection, 2 days for issuance, then 3 to 7 bank processing days.

## How am I refunded?

To the original payment method: the same bank card for a card payment, a bank transfer for cash on delivery, a new store credit if the original payment was a store credit. No cash refund is possible.

## Can I choose store credit instead of a refund?

Yes. The store credit is increased by 5 % of the refunded amount, remains valid for 12 months and can be used online and in store. It cannot be converted into cash.

## Are delivery fees refunded on a return?

No, unless the order is cancelled before shipment or the error is attributable to NovaShop (wrong, faulty or damaged product).

## Can I exchange a product instead of returning it?

Yes, for a product of equal or higher value, subject to stock availability. If the new product costs more, the difference is paid; if it costs less, the difference is refunded or converted into store credit.

## What happens if the returned product is found non-compliant?

The return is refused, the product is shipped back to the customer and the refund is not issued. The customer receives a written justification and may dispute it within 30 days.

## Can I return a product bought in store?

Yes, under the same time and eligibility conditions. In-store returns are free and the refund can be made on the spot or by transfer, depending on the original payment method.

## What should I do if my refund does not arrive?

If the refund does not appear 15 business days after validation, contact support with the order number and the return case number. A transfer receipt can be provided on request.
