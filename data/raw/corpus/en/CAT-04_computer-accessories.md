## 2. Compatibility

- **AC-401**: smartphones, tablets and laptops compatible with USB-C Power Delivery. Charges up to 65 W.
- **AC-402**: Windows, macOS, Linux and some Android devices with a USB-A port.
- **AC-403**: wired USB connection, compatible with Windows, macOS and Linux. AZERTY layout.
- **AC-404**: requires a USB-C port with DisplayPort Alt Mode for video output.
- **AC-406**: charges two devices simultaneously, maximum 20 W output per port.

## 3. Usage precautions

- Use a certified cable for fast charging; an uncertified cable limits the power delivered.
- Do not expose a power bank to temperatures above 40 °C.
- Disconnect the hub before carrying the laptop.
- Do not dismantle a charger: risk of electric shock and loss of warranty.

## 4. Sales conditions

6-month warranty on accessories, 3 months on cables and batteries. Change-of-mind return is possible within 14 days if the packaging is intact. Frayed or cut cables are not covered.

## 5. Frequently asked questions

**Does the 65 W charger charge a 100 W laptop?** It charges, but more slowly. For optimal charging, use a charger of equal or higher power.

**Does the mouse work on Mac?** Yes, via the USB receiver. The secondary button must be configured in system preferences.

**Does the hub get hot?** Slight warmth is normal under heavy use. Overheating with disconnection must be reported to support.

**Are the cables guaranteed against breakage?** The warranty covers manufacturing defects, not breakage from pulling or repeated folding.
