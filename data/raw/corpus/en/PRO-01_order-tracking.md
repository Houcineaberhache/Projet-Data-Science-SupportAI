# Procedure — Tracking your order

**Audience:** customer · **Duration:** 2 minutes

## 1. Where to find the order number

The order number appears in the confirmation email, in the format `ORD-` followed by 6 digits (example: `ORD-100234`). It is also visible in the customer account under "My orders".

## 2. Online tracking

1. Sign in at novashop.ma.
2. Open "My account" then "My orders".
3. Click on the relevant order.
4. Check the tracking timeline and the carrier tracking number.

## 3. Tracking without an account

A guest order can be tracked from the "Track my order" page by entering the order number **and** the email address used at purchase.

## 4. Understanding the statuses

| Displayed status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Awaiting payment | Order created, payment not confirmed |
| Payment confirmed | Payment validated, preparation pending |
| In preparation | Parcel being prepared in the warehouse |
| Shipped | Parcel handed to the carrier, tracking active |
| Out for delivery | Parcel on the delivery round |
| Delivered | Parcel handed to the customer |
| Return in progress | Parcel being returned to the warehouse |
| Cancelled | Order cancelled, refund according to POL-02 |

The full status reference is described in OPS-03.

## 5. Stuck tracking

If the status has not changed for more than **3 business days** while "Shipped", the customer may:

1. check tracking on the carrier website using the tracking number;
2. contact support with the order number;
3. request the opening of an investigation if the parcel cannot be located.

## 6. Tracking number not found

The tracking number is generated when the parcel is handed to the carrier. It may appear with a delay of a few hours. If it is missing 24 hours after the "Shipped" status, support regenerates the notification.

## 7. Changing the address during delivery

Once shipped, NovaShop can no longer change the address. The customer must contact the carrier directly using the tracking number. See POL-08, section 5.

## 8. Order delivered but not received

This is an incident, handled by POL-09, section 4. The customer must not open a new payment dispute before the carrier investigation is complete.

## 9. Times to know
