# FAQ — Orders and tracking

## How do I place an order?

Add the items to the basket, click "Checkout", enter your address and phone number, choose the delivery method and payment method, then confirm. You immediately receive a confirmation email containing your order number in the format `ORD-XXXXXX`.

## How can I check my order status?

Sign in to your customer account, open "My orders" and click on the relevant order. You will find the current status, the history and the carrier tracking number. If you ordered without creating an account, use the "Track my order" page with your order number and email address.

## I did not receive a confirmation email, is that normal?

The email usually arrives within a few minutes. Check your spam folder and the spelling of the address entered. If you still have not received it after 1 hour, contact support with your name and the order date. The order can be found even without the email.

## Can I modify my order after confirmation?

Yes, as long as the order has not been handed to the carrier. From "My orders", use the "Modify" button or contact support. After shipment, only a cancellation with return is possible. See POL-08.

## Can I cancel my order?

Yes, before shipment. Cancellation is free and the refund is full, including delivery fees. Once the parcel has been shipped, you must use the return procedure (POL-01).

## Why is my order still "In preparation"?

Preparation takes 2 to 24 business hours on average. A peak period (sales, holidays) may extend this to 48 hours. If the status does not change after 48 business hours, contact support.

## Can I order without creating an account?

Yes, guest checkout is available. You will receive tracking by email and SMS. Creating an account is still required to keep your history, invoices and loyalty points.

## How do I find an old order?

Orders from the **last 24 months** are kept in your customer account. Beyond that, contact support with the approximate date and the email address used. Invoices are kept for 10 years for accounting purposes.

## My order appears twice, is it a duplicate?

Check the order numbers: if they are identical, it is a simple display duplicate. If they differ, two orders were indeed recorded. Contact support immediately to cancel the second one before shipment.

## Can I order from abroad?

Delivery is limited to Moroccan territory. An order can be placed from abroad if the delivery address and payment method are Moroccan. Foreign cards may be declined by the issuing bank.

## What should I do if the status has not moved for several days?

A status stuck for more than 3 business days while "Shipped" justifies opening a carrier investigation. Contact support with the order number; an answer is provided within 48 business hours.
