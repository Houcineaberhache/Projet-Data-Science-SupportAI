# Regulatory summary — personal data protection

**Internal summary document. Not legal advice.**

## 1. Reference text

- **Law No. 09-08** on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data.
- Promulgated by dahir No. 1-09-15 of 18 February 2009.
- Published in **Official Bulletin No. 5714 of 5 March 2009**.
- Supervisory authority: **CNDP** (National Commission for the Control of Personal Data Protection).

## 2. Principles applicable to customer support

| Principle | Application at NovaShop |
| --- | --- |
| Determined purpose | Each collection is tied to a documented purpose (POL-06) |
| Proportionality | Collect only the data needed for processing |
| Limited retention | Retention periods defined per data type |
| Security and confidentiality | Encryption, role-based access, logging |
| Information of data subjects | Privacy policy accessible and readable |
| Consent | Required for marketing and non-essential cookies |
| Data subject rights | Access, rectification, objection, erasure |

## 3. Declaratory obligations

The processing of personal data is subject to formalities with the CNDP. Support must not state that a processing operation is "declared" or "compliant" without verification by the DPO.

**Point of attention:** the existence, number and scope of a CNDP declaration must be confirmed before any communication. No fictional declaration number may be published.

## 4. Sensitive data

The law strictly regulates sensitive data (health, origin, opinions, religion, biometric data). NovaShop does not collect any.

If a customer spontaneously communicates sensitive data in a support exchange, the agent must:

1. not record it in the ticket description field;
2. not reuse it;
3. refer the customer to the DPO if necessary.

## 5. Transfers and subcontracting

Any subcontractor processing data on behalf of NovaShop must provide sufficient guarantees. Transfers outside Morocco must be covered by appropriate contractual safeguards.

## 6. Data breach

In the event of a breach, the DPO is informed immediately. Notification to the CNDP and information of the data subjects may be required depending on severity.

## 7. What the assistant may say

- "Your data is processed in accordance with our privacy policy."
- "You may request access, rectification or erasure of your data by writing to dpo@novashop.ma."
- "Your request will be processed within 30 days."

## 8. What the assistant must not do

- Confirm the existence of a CNDP declaration.
- Disclose another customer's data.
- Request an identity document through an unsecured instant messaging channel.
- Give a legal interpretation of the law.
