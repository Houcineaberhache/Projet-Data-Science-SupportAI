# Product guide — Smartphones

This page presents the NovaShop smartphone range, the selection criteria and the associated conditions. Prices are in MAD, including all taxes, and may change.

| Reference | Model | Storage | Screen | Price | Warranty |

| SM-103 | NovaPhone Lite | 64 GB | 6.1" LCD | 1 299 MAD | 12 months |
| SM-104 | NovaPhone Max | 512 GB | 6.8" AMOLED 120 Hz | 5 499 MAD | 12 months |

## 2. Selection criteria

- **Budget**: Lite for essential use, X5 for versatile use, X5 Pro and Max for intensive use.
- **Photography**: the X5 Pro has a 50 MP main sensor with optical stabilisation.
- **Battery life**: 5 000 mAh on X5 and X5 Pro, 4 500 mAh on Lite, 5 500 mAh on Max.
- **Storage**: 64 GB is enough for light use; 256 GB or more is recommended for video and gaming.

## 3. Box contents

Each smartphone is supplied with: the device, a USB-C cable, a SIM ejector tool and documentation. The power adapter is not included on the Lite and X5 models, in line with the manufacturer's policy.

## 4. Network compatibility

All models are compatible with 2G, 3G and 4G on Moroccan networks. 5G is supported on X5 Pro and Max, subject to the operator's coverage. The models support dual SIM (nano-SIM + eSIM depending on the model).

## 5. Sales conditions

- 12-month commercial warranty (POL-04).
- Faulty on receipt: priority replacement within 48 hours (POL-09).
- Change-of-mind return possible within 14 days if the device has not been significantly used (POL-01).
- Network unlocking: all devices are sold without operator lock.

## 6. Care advice

Avoid prolonged exposure to sunlight, use a certified charger, and do not expose the device to moisture. A battery is best preserved between 20 % and 80 % charge.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**Is the charger included?** No on Lite and X5; yes on X5 Pro and Max.

**Can I use two SIM cards?** Yes, dual SIM across the range.

**Are updates guaranteed?** The manufacturer provides 2 years of security updates for the X5 range.

**Is a cracked screen covered?** No, accidental damage is excluded from the warranty (POL-04).
