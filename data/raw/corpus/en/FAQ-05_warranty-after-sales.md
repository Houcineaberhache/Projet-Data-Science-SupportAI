# FAQ — Warranty and after-sales

## How long is the product warranty?

12 months for smartphones, laptops, audio products, smartwatches and tablets. 6 months for computer accessories and 3 months for batteries and cables. The exact duration is stated on the invoice and the product page.

## Does the warranty cover a fall or a cracked screen?

No. Accidental damage (fall, impact, pressure, crack), liquid ingress, overvoltage and repairs carried out by a non-approved third party are not covered. A paid repair remains possible after a quote.

## Is the battery covered?

Yes, if its capacity drops below 80 % during the warranty period and in the absence of an exclusion cause. Normal wear related to age is not covered.

## How do I get my product repaired under warranty?

Open an after-sales case from the customer account under "My orders", then "Request after-sales support". You will receive a prepaid return label or a store appointment. Diagnosis is carried out within 5 business days.

## How long does a repair take?

48 hours for an accessory available in stock, 7 to 14 business days at the partner workshop, 14 to 21 business days if returned to the manufacturer. Beyond 21 business days, you may request a replacement or a refund.

## My product broke down within 48 hours, what should I do?

It is handled as "Dead On Arrival": priority replacement at no cost, subject to stock availability and verification of the fault. Contact support immediately with photos or a video of the problem.

## What happens if the product cannot be repaired?

NovaShop offers a store credit increased by 5 % or a refund proportional to the residual value, depending on how long the product has been used.

## Is the repair itself guaranteed?

Yes, the replaced part is guaranteed for 3 months. A service report stating the parts changed and the remaining warranty is provided with the product.

## Can I have my product repaired elsewhere without losing the warranty?

A repair by a non-approved third party ends the NovaShop commercial warranty for the damage concerned. Repairs outside the approved network are therefore not recommended during the warranty period.

## Is the warranty transferable if I resell the product?

The NovaShop commercial warranty is tied to the original invoice, not to the account holder. The new owner can benefit from it on presentation of the invoice, unless the manufacturer states otherwise.

## What should I do if I lose my invoice?

The order number or the account history serves as proof of purchase. Contact support to obtain a duplicate invoice.

## Is my warranty valid abroad?

The NovaShop commercial warranty applies in Morocco. Some manufacturers offer an international warranty; check the product page or contact support before travelling.
