# Product guide — Smartwatches and connected devices

## 2. Main features

- Heart rate, sleep and daily activity tracking.
- Call and message notifications.
- Built-in GPS on WR-501 only.
- Sport modes: running, cycling, swimming (WR-501), strength training.
- Contactless payment not available in Morocco on the range.

## 3. Compatibility

The watches are compatible with Android 8.0+ and iOS 14+. The companion app is required for synchronisation. Notifications depend on the permissions granted by the system.

## 4. Water resistance

| Rating | Use |
| --- | --- |
| 3 ATM | Resists splashes and rain |
| 5 ATM | Pool swimming, no diving |
| IP68 | Dust and short immersion |

Water resistance is not permanent: it may decrease as the seals wear. Hot baths and seawater are not recommended.

## 5. Battery life

The stated battery life is measured under standard use (notifications, activity tracking, medium brightness). GPS and always-on display significantly reduce battery life.

## 6. Sales conditions

12-month warranty excluding accidental damage and strap wear. Straps are considered consumables and are not covered beyond 30 days. Change-of-mind return requires an unworn product with the protective film intact.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**Can I swim with the NovaWatch SE?** It resists splashes, but swimming is not recommended (3 ATM).

**Does the GPS work without a phone?** Yes on WR-501. The other models use the phone's GPS.

**Can I change the strap?** Yes, standard 20 mm straps (22 mm on WR-503).

**Is the watch compatible with two phones?** No, one paired phone at a time.
