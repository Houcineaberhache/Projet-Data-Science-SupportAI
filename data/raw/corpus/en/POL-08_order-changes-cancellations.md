# Order cancellation and modification policy

**Version:** 1.0 · **Effective date:** 1 September 2026

## 1. Time window

An order can be cancelled or modified **as long as it has not been handed to the carrier**. In practice, the window is:

- 2 hours maximum after confirmation for orders placed during the day;
- until 22:00 the same day for orders placed before 16:00;
- no modification possible after the "Order shipped" email.

## 2. Statuses allowing cancellation

| Order status | Cancellation possible |
| --- | --- |
| Awaiting payment | Yes, immediately |
| Payment confirmed | Yes, before preparation |
| In preparation | Yes, if the parcel is not sealed |
| Shipped | No — use the return process (POL-01) |
| Delivered | No — use the return process (POL-01) |

## 3. Cancellation by the customer

The customer can cancel from the customer account under "My orders", using the "Cancel" button, or by contacting support. If payment has already been captured, the refund follows POL-02 and takes 3 to 7 business days.

No cancellation fee is applied before shipment.

## 4. Modifying an order

The following changes are possible before shipment:

- adding or removing an item;
- changing the delivery address;
- changing the phone number;
- switching from cash on delivery to card payment, or the reverse.

Changing an item may require a price adjustment and a new payment authorisation. Delivery fees are recalculated if the basket value crosses the free-shipping threshold.

## 5. Modification after shipment

Once shipped, the address can no longer be changed by NovaShop. The customer may:

- contact the carrier directly using the tracking number;
- refuse delivery, in which case the parcel is returned to the warehouse;
- request reshipping after return, charged 39 MAD.

## 6. Cancellation by NovaShop

NovaShop may cancel an order in the following cases:

- product unavailable after confirmation;
- obvious pricing or description error;
- suspected fraud or unauthorised payment;
- invalid delivery address or area not served.

In all cases, the customer is informed by email and fully refunded, including delivery fees, within 7 business days.

## 7. Non-cancellable orders

Personalised or engraved products enter production immediately and can no longer be cancelled after 1 hour. Digital products already activated cannot be cancelled.

## 8. Tracking the request

Each cancellation request creates a ticket visible in the customer account. Processing time is 4 business hours maximum. If refused, the reason is communicated in writing.
