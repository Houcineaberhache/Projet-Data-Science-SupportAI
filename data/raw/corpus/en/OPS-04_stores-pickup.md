Stores are closed on Sundays and national public holidays.

## 2. Services available in store

- Purchase, pickup and return of orders.
- After-sales drop-off and collection.
- Demonstration of display products.
- Payment in cash or by card.
- Collection of orders placed online (see section 3).

## 3. In-store pickup

In-store pickup is **free**. The customer selects this method at checkout, then chooses the store.

1. The order is prepared within 2 to 24 business hours.
2. The customer receives an email and an SMS containing a 6-digit pickup code.
3. The customer goes to the "Online pickup" desk with the code and an identity document.
4. The order is handed over after verification.

The collection window is **7 days** from notification. After that, the order is refunded or reshipped at the customer's request.

## 4. Pickup by a third party

Collection by another person is possible if the customer has designated a representative in the customer account. The representative must present the pickup code and their own identity document. Otherwise, collection is refused.

## 5. Dropping off a return in store

The customer may drop off a return in store free of charge, with the return authorisation (`RET-XXXXXX`) and the complete product. A drop-off receipt is issued. The quality inspection is carried out at the warehouse, except for accessories that can be checked immediately.

## 6. After-sales drop-off in store

The after-sales desk accepts products under warranty with the invoice or the order number. A drop-off receipt is issued, stating the visual condition of the product and the accessories handed over.

## 7. Services not available in store

- Modifying an order that has already been shipped.
- Cash refund of a card payment.
- Selling products that are not listed on the website.

## 8. Accessibility

The Casablanca and Rabat stores have step-free access. The Marrakech store is accessible by lift. Assistance can be requested at reception.
